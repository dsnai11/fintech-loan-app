Before you switch it on: the assistant only answers from the FAQ list below (and, if you allow it, a few facts about the customer's own loan). Make sure every answer there is true and approved by your compliance team. It always says it is an AI, it cannot decide or promise anything about a loan, and customers can reach your team at any time with "Talk to a person". If you connect an AI provider, say so in your privacy notice: the customer's message and a few loan facts are sent to that provider.
