How it works

The customer approves once in the app (UPI AutoPay or a bank mandate). A day before each due date they are told how much will be collected. On the due date the instalment is collected; if it fails it is tried again up to 3 times, a day apart, and the customer is told each time. Collected instalments are marked paid like any other payment. The customer can stop it any time in the app. Rules for mandates (maximum amount, notice period) come from your payment provider and RBI; confirm them with your compliance team.
Normally runs by itself every 30 minutes.
