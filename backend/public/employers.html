Credit head to confirm: how an employer is graded is your credit policy. This page only stores the list and lets the rules read it. An employer that is not on the list has no grade, and a rule of yours decides what that means.

The list

One employer per line: the name, a comma, then A, B or C. For example Tata Consultancy Services, A. Customers see the names as suggestions while they type where they work; they never see the grade. To act on the grade, add a rule of your own (Decisions, Rules tab, or Rules by product) that looks at "Employer category", where 3 = A, 2 = B, 1 = C and 0 = not on the list.
