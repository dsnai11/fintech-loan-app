Switching to Automatic sends real money without anyone pressing a button. Start with small limits and watch the list below for a few days. Automatic mode follows every rule: the limits you set, a bank account on file, no open high-risk alert, never the same payment twice. A payment that fails three times is left for a person. You can switch back to Manual at any time; anything already sent stays sent. Rewards and commissions that carry tax are always left for a person.

Who sends what

Manual means a person pays and enters the bank reference, on the page named under each item. Automatic means the system pays through your payment provider as soon as the rules are met (checked every 5 minutes) and records it for you.
