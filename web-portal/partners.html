Check with compliance and finance. The commission percentage is a cost to the company. A partner earns it once, on a customer's first loan paid out, and it is cancelled if the customer cancels in the cooling-off period. Whether tax is deducted from a commission is for your tax adviser; set the rate on the Accounting page. Commissions are never paid automatically: finance pays each one and enters the bank reference.
