Vintage

Loans grouped by the month they were paid out. Each box is the share of the money paid out that is late by at least the chosen number of days, measured at the end of each month after payout (month 1, month 2, and so on). A darker box is worse. If a group's boxes climb faster than older groups', lending from that month is performing worse.

Roll rate

Of the loans in each bucket on the first date, where were they on the second date. A loan "rolls" when it moves to a later bucket. Each row shows the share of loans (and of the rupees) that got worse.

Where customers came from

Customers who signed up in the dates below, by source and campaign, and how far each group got. Customers who sign up with a friend's code are counted as referrals; the rest as "app" unless the sign-up link carried a source.
